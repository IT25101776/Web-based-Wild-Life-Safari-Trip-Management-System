SAFARI LANKA - PROFILE, EXPERIENCE & VERIFIED REVIEWS UPDATE

1. PROFILE PICTURES
- Every logged-in role can open My Account and upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP profile picture.
- Maximum profile image size: 5 MB.
- Runtime files are saved under frontend/public/images/profiles/.
- If no picture exists, the dashboard shows the default user/profile icon.
- Clicking the Safari Lanka logo in every dashboard returns to the public home page.

2. TOURIST REGISTRATION VALIDATION
- Email must be a valid email address containing @.
- Phone must be exactly 10 digits and start with 0.
- Password must be at least 8 characters with at least one letter and one number.
- Password must be entered twice and both values must match.

3. EXPERIENCE PAGE
- Public navigation Experience now opens /experience.
- The page contains Yala wildlife experience cards and the supplied local wildlife photographs.
- Guest reviews are shown on the Experience page.
- /reviews provides the full public reviews page.

4. VERIFIED POST-TRIP REVIEWS
- Safari Operations can mark a confirmed, paid, fully assigned safari as COMPLETED on/after its visit date.
- Only the tourist who owns a COMPLETED booking can submit one review for that booking.
- Overall rating: required, 1-5.
- Driver rating: optional, 1-5.
- Guide rating: optional, 1-5.
- Driver/guide comments are optional.
- Tourist may upload up to 8 review media files.
- Images: JPG/PNG/WEBP, maximum 8 MB each.
- Videos: MP4/WEBM, maximum 50 MB each.
- Runtime review media is saved to frontend/public/reviews/media/.
- Submitted reviews are published to Experience and Reviews pages with the tourist name.

5. DATABASE
Fresh install: database/SAFARI_LANKA_FULL.sql
Existing database upgrade: database/UPGRADE_PROFILE_AND_REVIEWS.sql
Hibernate ddl-auto=update also creates the new model fields/table when the application starts.
