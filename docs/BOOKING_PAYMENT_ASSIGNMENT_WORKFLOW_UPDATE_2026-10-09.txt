Safari Lanka - Booking, Payment and Driver-Vehicle Workflow Update
Date: 2026-10-09

Implemented changes
===================
1. Sri Lankan tourist NIC validation
- New NIC: exactly 12 digits.
- Old NIC: exactly 9 digits followed by V (case-insensitive input, stored uppercase).
- Applied to tourist registration, My Account and booking visitor details, with matching backend validation.

2. Operator confirmation before payment
- New tourist bookings start PENDING + UNPAID.
- Tourist payment is locked until Safari Operations changes the booking to CONFIRMED.
- Tourist Dashboard and Payment page explain that the booking is waiting for operator confirmation.
- Card payment becomes PAID immediately after successful prototype payment.
- Bank transfer becomes PENDING until Finance approves it.
- Safari resource assignment requires booking CONFIRMED and payment PAID.

3. Booking cancellation reason
- Safari Operator cancellation requires a reason.
- Saved to bookings.cancellation_reason.
- Shown to Tourist, Safari Operator and System Administrator in booking details.

4. Payment rejection reason
- Finance rejection requires a reason.
- Saved to payments.rejection_reason.
- Payment/booking payment status becomes REJECTED.
- Shown to Tourist and Safari Operator (and Admin) in relevant details.
- Tourist may submit payment again after rejection while booking remains CONFIRMED.

5. Safari Guide account fields
- Driving licence removed from Safari Guide creation/edit/profile requirements.
- Driving licence remains required only for Jeep Driver accounts.

6. Same-date assignment conflict protection
- Drivers, guides and their linked vehicles already assigned to a trip on a date are excluded from another booking on the same date.
- Backend validation also blocks conflicts even if a frontend request is manipulated.

7. Admin vehicle management
- System Administrator can create/edit vehicles from Vehicle Management.
- Driver account creation/editing supports assigning an available vehicle.
- Changing a driver's vehicle relinks the vehicle and removes the previous link.

8. Automatic vehicle assignment
- Safari Operations selects only Jeep Driver + Safari Guide.
- The vehicle linked to the selected driver is assigned automatically.
- The linked vehicle is shown before confirmation.
- Driver, guide and vehicle availability is revalidated by the backend.

Database changes
================
- bookings.cancellation_reason VARCHAR(1000)
- payments.rejection_reason VARCHAR(1000)
- REJECTED added to payment status checks for bookings and payments.
- visitor age check aligned to 1..99.
- Existing vehicles.driver_id is used for Driver-to-Vehicle association; no new relationship table is required.
